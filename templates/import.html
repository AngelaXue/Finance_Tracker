Bring in older history (optional)

Years of past transactions from a spreadsheet or another app

Banks usually only offer a year or two of CSVs. If you've kept older records in a spreadsheet, reshape them into this layout and bring them in once — it's safe to run again (rows already here are skipped).

  • Transactions template — Date, Account, Amount required (Amount negative = money out); Description, Category, Type, Method, Note and Balance optional.
  • Accounts template (optional) — creates accounts that don't exist yet.
  • Balances template (optional) — known balances on given dates.
  • A workbook (.xlsx) works too: sheets named Transactions, Accounts, Balances.

Which banks work?

  • Most of them. Any CSV with a date column and an amount column (or Debit / Credit columns) is read automatically — Bank of America and Chase have dedicated readers.
  • Look for "Download", "Export" or "Statements" on the account's page, and pick CSV (sometimes called "Spreadsheet" or "Excel CSV"). Choose a date range that overlaps a little with your last import — overlaps are flagged, not lost.
  • Signs look wrong after Preview? (purchases showing as money in) — set that file to Flip all signs. To make it permanent for that bank, describe its layout in config/csv_formats.json — see the README.
  • Can't be read at all? Usually a day-first date or a file without a header row — the same config file handles those.
