Set Balance

  • Accounts marked from transactions have no recorded balance, so their balance is the sum of all their transactions — right if the first one was the opening deposit.
  • If that's off (e.g. the account had money before you started logging), enter what your bank shows for a date — today is easiest. Transactions on or before that date are treated as already included; later ones are added on top.
  • Credit cards and loans: enter what you owe as a positive number. Setting the same account + date again replaces it. Brokerage/retirement balances are set on the Investments page.
