How contributions are logged automatically

  • On Transactions, a transfer to your portfolio has Type investment_contribution (on the bank account the money left).
  • It goes to the account named in its note — {% for s in sections %}{{ s.name.split()[0]|lower }} → {{ s.name }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %} (capitals don't matter) — or, if the note doesn't say, its description (e.g. "Transfer to Roth IRA"). A choice made in a "Went to" / "Change…" dropdown here beats both.
  • It then adds to that account's principal and value right away. Value is only an estimate until your next current value, which replaces it with the real number (market gains/losses included).
  • Money that never went through a logged transaction: use Principal added in the form above.
  • Anything with an investment Category, or that can't be matched, shows up in the list near the top until it's sorted out.
