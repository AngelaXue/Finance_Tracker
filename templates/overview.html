Getting started

Four steps to your first real numbers. Everything stays on this computer.

  1. Add your accounts — checking, savings, cards, investments. Net Worth → Add Account {% if setup.accounts %}({{ setup.accounts }} added ✓){% endif %}
  2. Import statements — download a CSV from each bank's website and drop it on the Import page. Have years of history in a spreadsheet? That can come in too (optional).
  3. Review categories on Transactions — yellow rows were filled in automatically and need a quick look. Every correction makes the next suggestions better.
  4. Explore — Cash Flow, Spending, Investments. Help explains every rule.
